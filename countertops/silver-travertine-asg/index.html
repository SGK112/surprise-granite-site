Silver Travertine Countertops

Silver Travertine is a striking natural limestone characterized by its sophisticated blend of cool greys, silvers, and subtle beige undertones. Primarily sourced from major quarries in Turkey, Italy, and Iran, this sedimentary rock is defined by its porous texture and distinctive veining. Its neutral palette allows it to integrate seamlessly into modern designs, appearing in applications ranging from sleek interior flooring to textured wall cladding. Depending on the cut, it reveals either linear streaks or a clouded, organic movement. Silver Travertine is a versatile natural stone suitable for a wide range of interior and exterior applications.

About Silver
