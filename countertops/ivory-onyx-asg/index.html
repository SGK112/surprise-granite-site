About Ivory Onyx

Ivory Onyx is a premium natural stone quarried in Turkey, prized for its luminous, creamy white backdrop and delicate veining. Unlike darker varieties, it features a translucent quality that allows light to pass through, making it an ideal choice for backlit feature walls, countertops, and decorative accents. The stone typically displays soft layers of beige, gold, or light grey, offering a sophisticated and airy aesthetic. Its smooth, polished finish provides a high-end feel that enhances modern and classic interior designs alike. Ivory Onyx is a versatile natural stone suitable for a wide range of interior and exterior applications.
