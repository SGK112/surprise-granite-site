About Fantasy Ocean

Fantasy Ocean is a marble that is sourced in India. Due to its hardness, it is well suited for kitchen countertop use. This marble has horizontal linear movement that is blue, grey, and white in color. Random thick, black veins show up throughout this marble, creating a dramatic look.

Every Fantasy Ocean installation includes digital templating, professional fabrication, and expert installation by our Arizona-based team. Pricing is per project and depends on square footage, edge profile, and cutouts — estimate your project or book a free in-home measure for exact numbers.
