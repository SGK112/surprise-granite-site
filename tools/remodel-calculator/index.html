Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Phoenix?

Phoenix bathroom remodels range from about $8,000 for a shower or vanity refresh to $30,000+ for a full renovation with new tile, shower, vanity, countertop, and flooring. Most mid-range remodels land in the $12,000–$25,000 range.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A shower or vanity update takes a few days to a week. A full bathroom renovation typically runs 2–4 weeks depending on scope, tile work, and plumbing changes.
