See New Countertops in Your Own Kitchen — Before You Buy

Picking a countertop from a small sample is hard. Our free AI room visualizer lets you upload a photo of your real kitchen or bathroom and preview new granite or quartz countertops, a tile backsplash, and flooring swapped right into your space. See the whole look come together in seconds — no guesswork, no showroom trip required.

How the Room Visualizer Works

  1. Upload a photo — a straight-on, well-lit shot of your kitchen or bath works best.
  2. Pick your materials — choose a countertop color, then optionally add a tile backsplash and flooring from our libraries.
  3. Let AI do the swap — our AI renders the new surfaces into your actual room so you can judge the color, contrast, and overall feel.
  4. Get a real quote — love it? Request a free in-home estimate and we'll price your exact space.

What You Can Visualize

Mix and match across every surface that defines a remodel: countertops (granite, quartz, marble, quartzite), tile backsplashes and accent walls, and flooring (tile, wood-look, and stone-look). It's the fastest way to settle the "will this color work with my cabinets?" question before committing to a slab.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI room visualizer work?

Upload a straight-on photo of your kitchen or bathroom, choose a countertop color plus optional tile and flooring, and our AI swaps the new materials into your actual room so you can see the look before you commit. It's free and takes under a minute.

Is the countertop visualizer free?

Yes — completely free, no signup. Preview as many countertop, tile, and flooring combinations as you like, then request a free in-home estimate when you're ready.

Are the visualized materials accurate?

The AI preview is for inspiration and narrowing down a look. Natural stone veining is unique to each slab, so exact materials, veining, and layout are confirmed on your free in-home measure — order a sample or visit a local stone yard for the truest color.

What photos work best?

Straight-on, well-lit shots where the countertops, backsplash, and floor are clearly visible. JPEG and PNG are supported.
