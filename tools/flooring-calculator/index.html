Frequently Asked Questions

How much flooring do I need for a room?

Multiply room length × width in feet for square footage, then add 7–10% for waste and cuts (more for diagonal or plank layouts). This calculator handles the waste factor for you.

How much does flooring installation cost in Phoenix?

Installed flooring in the Phoenix area runs roughly $5–$15 per square foot depending on material and subfloor prep — tile and stone at the higher end, LVP at the lower end.
