What dekton is

Sintered stone made under extreme pressure and heat. Effectively immune to heat, UV, scratching and staining.

What to watch for

Hard enough to be brittle at the edges during fabrication, and priced accordingly. It is the durable option, not the cheap one.

Who it suits

Outdoor kitchens and anyone who will genuinely put a hot pan straight down.
