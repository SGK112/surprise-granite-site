What quartzite is

Natural stone, metamorphosed sandstone. Harder than granite and often looks like marble, which is exactly why people want it.

What to watch for

Sold loosely. Some slabs labelled quartzite are softer dolomitic marble that will etch. Ask for the acid test before you commit.

Who it suits

People who want the marble look without the marble maintenance, and will pay for it.
