What marble is

Classic, and the reason every high-end kitchen photo looks the way it does. Cool to the touch, which is why pastry chefs insist on it.

What to watch for

It etches. Lemon juice, vinegar and wine dull the polish where they land, and no sealer prevents it. This is not a defect, it is what marble does.

Who it suits

People who genuinely like patina, or who bake. Not for anyone who will be upset by the first ring.

Common questions

How much do marble countertops cost per square foot?

$74 to $166 installed, based on 136 marble slabs priced today. Material alone is $19–$111; fabrication and installation add about $55 per square foot.

How much is a marble kitchen?

A typical 45 square foot kitchen in marble runs $3,315 to $7,448 installed. Bigger kitchens and islands push that up; a waterfall edge or a mitred edge adds meaningfully more.

Is marble worth it?

People who genuinely like patina, or who bake. Not for anyone who will be upset by the first ring. It etches. Lemon juice, vinegar and wine dull the polish where they land, and no sealer prevents it. This is not a defect, it is what marble does.
