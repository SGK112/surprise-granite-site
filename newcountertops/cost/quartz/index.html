What quartz is

Ground natural quartz bound in resin, made in a factory to a spec. Non-porous, so it never needs sealing and cannot stain from wine or oil.

What to watch for

Heat is the weakness. The resin scorches, so a pan straight off the burner can leave a mark that does not come out. Use a trivet and it will outlive the kitchen.

Who it suits

Anyone who wants to stop thinking about their countertops after installation day.
