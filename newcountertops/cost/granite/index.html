What granite is

Quarried stone, cut into slabs. Every slab is different, which is the appeal and the reason to look at the actual slab rather than a sample chip.

What to watch for

It is porous. It wants sealing every year or two, and a lemon left overnight can etch a dull patch. Cheaper than quartz to buy, marginally more to live with.

Who it suits

People who want real stone and are happy that no two kitchens look the same.
