What porcelain is

Large-format sintered slab, typically thin. Extremely hard, completely non-porous, and UV-stable.

What to watch for

The colour is often surface-printed, so a chipped edge can show a paler body underneath. Fabrication needs someone who has done it before.

Who it suits

Outdoor kitchens, waterfall edges, and anywhere a thin profile matters.
