Privacy policy

The short version: we collect what you type into the quote form, we use it to price your kitchen and get a licensed fabricator to you, and we don't sell it to anyone else.

Effective 8 September 2026 · applies to newcountertops.com only

Who we are. NewCountertops.com is operated by Surprise Granite, a licensed Arizona contractor (ROC #367593). When you request a quote inside our Arizona service area, Surprise Granite does the work. Outside it, we hand you to one licensed local fabricator — which is the one time your details leave us, and section 3 says exactly what goes.

1. What we collect

When you fill in the quote form, we collect only what the form asks for:

  • Your ZIP code, the rough square footage of the countertops, the material you're leaning toward, and your timeline.
  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • The price range we showed you, and a timestamped record that you ticked the consent box.

We do not ask for, and have no use for, your date of birth, your social security number, your income or any payment details. Nothing on this site takes a payment.

When you simply read the site, two ordinary things happen:

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 (property G-9HJRRMG310, shared with surprisegranite.com) on the cost, calculator and quote pages. It records which pages you viewed, roughly where you are, and what kind of device you're on. It sets cookies in your browser to do that.
  • Server logs. Our host (Render) and our CDN (Cloudflare) record the usual request data — IP address, timestamp, page requested, browser — to serve the site and keep it standing up.

2. What we use it for

  • Working out your price range and, if you ask for one, producing a real quote.
  • Contacting you about that quote by phone, text or email — which is what the consent box on the form is for.
  • Routing your request to whoever can actually do the work.
  • Understanding, in aggregate, which pages are useful and which are not.

We do not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we don't add you to a newsletter you didn't ask for.

3. Who we share it with

This is the part worth reading properly, because it's the whole point of the site.

If your ZIP code is in our Arizona service area (roughly 85001–86556), your request goes to Surprise Granite and nowhere else. The people who call you are the people who will cut and fit the stone.

If it isn't, we share your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code and the project details you gave us with one licensed countertop fabricator near you, so they can quote the job. One — not an auction, and not a list.

We do not sell your information to data brokers, lead-aggregation networks or anyone else, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Beyond that, your information reaches only the service providers we need to run the business — our hosting and email providers, and Google Analytics as described above — and anyone we're legally required to give it to.

4. Calls, texts and how to stop them

Ticking the consent box on the quote form is your agreement to be contacted about that quote by phone, text or email, including by autodialer or pre-recorded message. Consent is not a condition of purchase — you can price a kitchen on this site without ever giving us a phone number, and the calculator asks for nothing at all. Message and data rates may apply.

To stop it: reply STOP to any text, say so on any call, or email info@surprisegranite.com. Opting out of marketing contact doesn't stop us replying to a quote you actively asked for, but you can ask us to close that out too.

5. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you're in California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut or another state with a consumer privacy law, those are your statutory rights and we apply them to everyone rather than checking your address first. You can also ask us not to sell or share your information — though as section 3 says, we don't.

Email info@surprisegranite.com with the phrase "privacy request" and the email address or phone number you gave us. We'll respond within 45 days, and we won't treat you any differently for asking.

To opt out of Google Analytics specifically, you can install Google's opt-out browser add-on, which works across every site that uses it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as we're working with you and afterwards as a business record — we need to know what we quoted and when. Ask us to delete it and we will, except where we're required to keep a record (a completed contract, for instance).

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and quote submissions are encrypted in transit. Access to lead records is limited to the people at Surprise Granite who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we won't pretend otherwise — but we hold very little about you, which is the most effective protection there is.

8. Children

This site is for homeowners buying countertops. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us something, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll change the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects what we do with information already collected, we'll say so on this page rather than quietly editing it.
