Countertops, cabinets & remodeling across the Phoenix metro

Surprise Granite is a licensed Arizona contractor (ROC #341113) fabricating and installing granite, quartz, quartzite, and marble countertops from $55/sqft — plus custom cabinets, tile, luxury vinyl flooring, and full kitchen & bath remodels. We're a service-area business: you shop products online or pick your slab at a partner yard, and we come to you for a free in-home measure and estimate across Surprise, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, and the wider Maricopa County.

Frequently asked questions

Does Surprise Granite have a showroom open to the public?
No. Surprise Granite is a service-area contractor headquartered at 15464 W Aster Dr in Surprise, AZ — we are not open for walk-ins. Customers select countertop slabs at our partner stone yards across the Phoenix metro (Arizona Tile, MSI, Cosentino, and others), and we come to your home for free in-home estimates and measurements.
What areas around Phoenix does Surprise Granite serve?
We serve the entire Phoenix metropolitan area in Maricopa County, Arizona — including Surprise, Peoria, Glendale, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Litchfield Park, Sun City, Sun City West, and Anthem. In-home estimates are free across this service area.
How much do new countertops cost in Phoenix, AZ?
For a typical Phoenix-metro kitchen (about 40 sq ft of counter), installed pricing in 2026 runs $3,000–$7,000 for granite, $3,500–$6,500 for quartz, and $5,500–$11,000 for quartzite. Cabinet refacing averages $4,500–$9,000. All quotes include in-home measurement, fabrication in our Surprise shop, edge profile, sink cutout, and installation.
Do you do cabinet installation, tile, and stone — or just countertops?
All of it. Surprise Granite installs custom cabinets and cabinet refacing, ceramic and porcelain tile, natural stone (travertine, marble, slate, limestone, quartzite) flooring, tile showers and tub-to-shower conversions, and custom millwork — in addition to countertop fabrication and installation. We are licensed AZ ROC #341113, bonded and insured, and do full kitchen and bath remodels under one roof.
How fast can you install new countertops or cabinets?
Standard countertop projects take 2–3 weeks from template to install. Cabinet refacing averages 3 weeks; semi-custom kitchen cabinets 5–7 weeks; fully custom 6–10 weeks. Tile showers run 7–10 days. We schedule the in-home estimate within 48 hours of your call.
Is Surprise Granite licensed and insured in Arizona?
Yes. Licensed Arizona ROC #341113 (Arizona Registrar of Contractors), bonded, insured, and our installation crews are background-checked. We are 4.6 stars across 400+ Google reviews.
