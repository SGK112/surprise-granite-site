Frequently Asked Questions

How much do granite countertops cost in Avondale?

Granite countertops in Avondale typically run $45–$80 per square foot installed, depending on the color and slab. An average 30 sq ft kitchen runs about $1,500–$2,700 with fabrication, edge, and installation included.

Do granite countertops need sealing in Arizona?

Yes — we seal your granite on installation, and a quick re-seal every 1–2 years keeps it stain-resistant. Arizona's dry climate is easy on stone; the main thing is wiping spills and resealing on schedule.

Is granite or quartz better for a Avondale kitchen?

Granite gives you natural, one-of-a-kind stone and top heat resistance; quartz gives you a consistent pattern and no sealing. Both are excellent in Avondale — we'll show you both and help you choose.
