Quartzite in Litchfield Park typically runs $60–$120 per square foot installed, in the premium tier alongside high-end granite and quartz.

Is quartzite the same as quartz?

No — quartzite is 100% natural stone (like granite), while quartz is engineered. Quartzite gives you natural veining and needs sealing; quartz is man-made and doesn't. We carry both and can compare them side by side for your Litchfield Park project.
