When to Replace

Signs It's Time to Replace Your Countertops

If any of these sound familiar, replacement is usually more cost-effective than one more repair.

Cracks & Chips

Cracks around the sink or cooktop, or chipped edges and corners, only spread over time — and they can't be fully hidden with filler.

Stains & Burns

Etch marks on marble, scorch marks from hot pans, or stains that no longer clean out mean the surface has lost its finish and seal.

Dated Laminate or Tile

Peeling laminate, cracked tile grout, and 1990s colors drag down your whole kitchen — and your home's resale value.

Separated Seams

Seams that have lifted or opened up collect water and grime and signal the tops are at the end of their life.

Won't Hold a Seal

If you're resealing constantly and water still soaks in, the stone is worn past the point that maintenance can fix.

Kitchen Remodel

New cabinets, a new sink, or a full kitchen remodel is the perfect time to replace the countertops in the same trip.
